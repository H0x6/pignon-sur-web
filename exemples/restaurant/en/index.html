The house

A forty-seat dining room with green velvet banquettes and old mirrors, and a twenty-seat terrace under the arcades as soon as the sun is out.

In the kitchen, Thomas cooks with vegetables from Sundgau growers, river fish and meat from nearby farms. In the dining room, Léa will suggest a wine from Alsace or further afield. Everything is homemade, from the bread to the desserts.

Thomas and Léa Muller

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book?

In the evening and on Sundays, it is a good idea. At weekday lunchtimes there is often a free table.

Can you host a group?

Yes, up to 25 people, with a menu chosen together in advance. Give us a call.

Are there vegetarian dishes?

Always at least one, and the kitchen is happy to adapt a dish on request.
